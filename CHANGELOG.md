# Changelog

What changed in each Bluebox release, newest first. Each version ships as one build for iPhone, iPad and Mac, on the [Releases](https://github.com/BCYAYAY14/bluebox/releases) page and through the AltStore source.

## 1.2.6 (31 October 2026)

### Remote control
- The Mac's window scrolls to follow the highlight, so moving down a list or a page keeps what's highlighted on screen.

### Player
- Fixed the Picture in Picture window on iPhone and iPad flashing grey and darkening every few seconds while a film played.

## 1.2.5 (31 October 2026)

### Remote control
- Swipes and Back keep working while a stream opens; Back on the iPhone cancels the loading screen.
- The highlight and pointer no longer stay drawn over the film or the loading screen, and come back on the same item when you return.
- Moving the highlight is quicker, and the ring stays on its item after the page scrolls or a new page opens.
- The iPhone switches to the player's controls more reliably while the Mac plays.
- Swipes keep to the row or column they started in, a flick after a long swipe no longer overshoots, and the pointer speeds up with a quick finger.

### Player
- Stats for nerds is far more detailed: a live graph of bitrate, buffer and download speed, and sections for video (with HDR, colour and bit depth), audio, subtitles, the stream, frames, the buffer, the torrent and sync.
- A timeline of what happened during playback: opening, buffering and how long, stalls, seeks, track changes and errors.
- Copy Diagnostics puts a report on the clipboard for bug reports, with links, keys, addresses and server names left out.
- Fixed the film carrying on playing after you paused it, on streams played through VLC.

### Website
- A documentation site with install steps, guides to sources, the player, the iPhone remote, Watch Party, profiles, sync and privacy, and a Help and FAQ page for when something goes wrong.

## 1.2.4 (31 October 2026)

### Watch Party
- Opening an invite shows an invitation instead of a form: who invited you, what's on, who's already there and ready, and whether the party is in the lobby or already playing. On the Mac it fills the window over the film's backdrop; on iPhone it's a full screen.
- Before you join, it checks your own sources for the same copy, so you know you'll be able to watch along.
- Joining as your profile, with Change for a name and colour just for this party.
- One Join Party button turns the invitation into the lobby, or opens the player at the party's moment if it's already playing.
- A locked or ended party, or a host who isn't answering, is shown on the invitation with Try Again.
- Join a Party… has eight code boxes, and pasting an invite link anywhere fills them.
- Share Invite… sends a web link that opens a small join page, so friends without Bluebox see who invited them and where to get it.

### Remote control
- On iPhone the remote opens full screen, so swiping down on the touch pad, with the highlight or the pointer, no longer closes it. The arrow at the top closes it.

## 1.2.3 (31 October 2026)

### Remote control
- One remote instead of two tabs. Above the touch pad, the iPhone shows what's highlighted on the Mac with the items either side of it and the row it's in, so you can drive without looking up.
- There's always a highlight: after a while it dims to an outline instead of disappearing, and a new page starts on its first item, so every swipe moves and every tap presses something.
- Everything you could click on the Mac can be reached: the sidebar, TV Shows and Movies, More Info and My List, See All, the search field, Downloads, every Settings row and switch, and the buttons in sheets.
- New gestures: a fast swipe jumps several items, holding at the edge keeps moving, holding opens an item's menu on both screens, two fingers scroll (and coast), and a swipe in from the left edge goes back.
- A pointer, with a two-finger tap or Use a Pointer, for clicking anything. It comes on by itself on a screen with nothing to highlight.
- At the end of what's showing, a swipe scrolls the page on and carries on.
- While something plays: Skip Intro and Next Episode appear on the iPhone when the Mac offers them, the timeline shows chapters, and circling round the pad's edge scrubs in fine steps.

## 1.2.2 (31 October 2026)

### Remote control
- Taps on the iPhone's touch pad no longer press the title page's Play button again once the film is playing. While the Mac plays, a tap plays or pauses and a swipe left or right skips 10 seconds.
- While a stream is loading, the touch pad waits, so a second tap can't start it twice.
- When nothing is highlighted on the Mac, the first tap or swipe shows the highlight: on whatever is under the Mac's pointer, or the first thing on screen. The next tap opens it.
- Sheets and overlays, like a trailer, Download Rules or the season list, keep the touch pad off the page underneath, and Back closes them.
- A page with another opened over it can't be reached by mistake, and the remote shows the highlighted item's current label, like Resume.
- A swipe the iPhone interrupts no longer leaves the touch pad stuck.

## 1.2.1 (31 October 2026)

### Settings
- A link to another page, like Language on Appearance, now opens that page properly: the Mac's sidebar moves to it, and on iPhone the back button comes back.
- The Appearance preview shows the app icon on the Mac.
- Every live preview uses the same sunset scene.
- On Subtitles, each site keeps its key, sign-in and switches together. OpenSubtitles, Subdl and debrid keys all save the same way, and the debrid key no longer saves on every keystroke.
- Each section says whose settings it holds, the same way on every page: only for you, or everyone on this device.
- Clearer wording throughout, merged duplicate sections, and every setting appears in one place and in search.

### Menus and sheets
- Right-click or touch and hold now opens Bluebox's own menu everywhere: downloads (with Show Episodes for a show), the debrid library, local files, recent searches, saved subtitle looks, the release list on the Mac, party chat and the player's Next Episode button.
- Jump to Time is a short sheet whose example fits the video, and it says why Go is greyed out.
- Join a Party is a standard form, with Cancel and Join at the top.
- Deleting downloads, a season or a list, and downloading a whole collection, ask in Bluebox's own dialog.
- Profile pictures are the same shape everywhere, and Search's filter chips show a choice in the theme colour.

### Remote control
- Picking your Mac in the iPhone's Play On menu opens the remote straight away, even when nothing is playing, and the menu has Open Remote.

## 1.2.0 (31 October 2026)

### Watch Party
- Start a party from Play's ▾ or any title's right-click menu, and watch in step with friends on your Bluebox server. Friends join with a link, or with a code in File › Join a Party….
- A chat beside the video on the Mac and iPad, and over or under it on iPhone, with replies, mentions and quick reactions that float up over everyone's video.
- A lobby with ready checks and a 3, 2, 1. Everyone's own copy is found before it starts, and the party pauses while someone catches up.
- Up next and a party queue, Step Away and Catch Up, and host tools to lock the party, hand it over or end it.

### Remote control
- A new remote on iPhone, with a touch pad, and it controls the whole Mac app: browse, open a title and play, not just the player.
- Seeking from the remote works, and the audio and subtitle buttons answer at once. Commands are confirmed and resent if lost, and the remote shows whether it's connected.
- Play On sends what you pick to the Mac.

### Finding things to watch
- Your add-ons' own catalogs can be rows on Home, each with a See All page and its own switch.
- Collection pages: a title's "Part of …" row opens the whole collection, with Play All, Download All and Add All to My List.
- Search understands years ("dune 2021"), Esc and ✕ clear it, Clear Filters appears when a filter is on, and Clear All has Undo. Search history can be turned off in Settings › Privacy.
- Widgets for Continue Watching and Up Next, Siri and Shortcuts, and your library in Spotlight.

### Sources
- Plex and WebDAV folders. Emby joins Jellyfin, and watched progress now syncs both ways with both.
- Importing files and folders works again: a folder that moves is kept with an error instead of vanishing, more video types are found, dropping several items imports them all, and there's File › Open… (⌘O).
- Fix Match corrects a file Bluebox matched to the wrong title.
- AllDebrid and Debrid-Link have the cloud library and cache check. Settings shows how many days your debrid plan has left, with a reminder three days before it ends.
- When a debrid service sends its error clip instead of the film, Bluebox says why and offers to open the service or pick another release.

### Playback
- Open Videos In: play in VLC, IINA, mpv or Infuse on the Mac, and Infuse, VLC, Outplayer, nPlayer, SenPlayer or VidHub on iPhone and iPad.
- AirPlay under VLC explains why it's greyed out and offers a release AirPlay can play.
- The next episode is found in the background, so it starts sooner.
- On iPhone, swipe to seek, and up and down for brightness and volume.

### Kids and accounts
- Settings, and editing your own restricted profile, ask for the PIN. Kids profiles block unrated titles unless Allow Unrated is on.
- Signing out warns you when changes haven't been uploaded yet, and a failed Undo offers Try Again.
- Trakt custom lists can sync (off by default).

### Look and language
- Choose Bluebox's colour from ten. The app icon and Dock icon follow it.
- Bluebox in Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese, chosen in Settings › Language.
- Settings is grouped into sections, with a line under each page's title saying what's there.
- One set of animations throughout, which respect Reduce Motion.

## 1.1.2 (30 October 2026)

### Subtitles
- Sync to the Audio no longer crashes Bluebox the first time it asks to use speech recognition.
- Sync to the Audio is quicker and surer. It listens to up to three parts of the film, getting the next ready while it works on the last, and stops after two if they agree. It also knows the names and unusual words in the subtitles before it listens.
- A sync carries on when you close the card, and the player tells you when it's done, with Undo.
- Subtitle files keep their timing at any speed. At 2× and above, lines used to come up late, and short ones could be missed.
- A line no longer flashes up early and disappears again for a moment, and lines in the video without an end time no longer sit on top of the next one.

### Playback
- With the system player, voices keep their pitch at every speed from 0.25× to 4×, instead of going silent below 0.5× and above 2×.
- The messages over the video (volume, speed, skipped intro, saved frame) have the player cards' look, with an icon, a level bar for volume, and Undo set apart. Pressing a key again updates the message instead of bringing it in again.

## 1.1.1 (30 October 2026)

### Playback
- A frozen picture fixes itself. Resuming something partway through could leave the picture stuck while the sound played on; Bluebox now notices within a few seconds and restarts the picture where you are.
- VLC playback is updated, with fixes for seeking, pausing and recovering H.264 video.

### Menus
- Right-click (or touch and hold) on an episode opens Bluebox's own menu, like every poster, instead of the system one.
- The ⋯ on a Continue Watching card no longer closes its menu the moment it opens.

## 1.1.0 (30 October 2026)

### Subtitles
- Subtitles stay where you drop them. Before, letting go while the controls showed lifted them straight back up, so a move looked like it had been undone.
- Drag subtitles into the black bar under a letterboxed picture, or the one above it, to keep them off the picture. This works for subtitle files and the lines Bluebox draws; text VLC draws into the picture stays on it.
- With a letterboxed video, subtitles move up only as far as the controls actually cover the picture, not as if the black bar were part of it.

## 1.0.16 (30 October 2026)

### Subtitles
- Subtitle Style opens as a panel beside the video, and the picture moves over to make room, so every change shows on the real video.
- New presets: Standard, Cinema, Boxed, Bold Outline and Large Print. They set the font and weight too. Save Current keeps a look of your own as a preset, synced with your profile.
- Choose the font in the player, from a list that shows each font in itself. Bluebox now includes Atkinson Hyperlegible and Inter, so subtitles look the same on every device. Regular, Semibold or Bold.
- Position: drag the subtitles on a small picture (or on the video) and they snap to Top, Middle or Bottom. Middle now stays Middle.
- While the controls show, subtitles move up just far enough to clear them, the video's own tracks included, and never jump. Paused, the controls fade after a few seconds like they do while playing, and the subtitles settle back.
- Settings › Subtitles uses the same presets, fonts and position picture as the player.

### Player
- An equaliser in Tools › Sound: ten bands you drag, presets (Voice, Bass Boost, Treble, Cinema, Music, Headphones), and Preamp. It's your profile's, plays with every video, and is also in Settings › Playback. HLS streams play without it.
- Night Mode works with VLC (MKV and most torrents). Before, it had no effect there. Turning it on or off no longer reopens the video, and Dialogue Boost with VLC is no longer quieter than it should be.
- Picture has Frame and Colour tabs. Long lists of choices (aspect, crop, sleep timer, speed) are segmented controls with More for the rarer ones, instead of rows of pills.
- On the Mac the switches in the player's cards are switches, not checkboxes. On iPhone the cards no longer cover the title, and every button is at least 44 points.

## 1.0.15 (30 October 2026)

### Subtitles
- Bluebox now draws plain subtitle tracks inside MKV and WebM files itself, instead of VLC. They follow every style change straight away, can be dragged and pinched, and can be the second line. Styled ASS (signs, karaoke) and picture subtitles from Blu-rays and DVDs stay with VLC.
- Subtitle files that come with a torrent are listed under their language, tagged Torrent. One is picked for you when the video has no track in your language.
- Subtitles show in Picture in Picture and on AirPlay, in your own style.
- A Lines page beside Style and Timing lists every line with its time. Search it, or click a line to play from there.
- Sync to the Audio, at the top of Timing, listens to the speech and lines the subtitles up for you, on your device. It asks for speech recognition the first time.
- Hide Sound Cues, on the Subtitle Style page, leaves out [siren wailing], ♪ lyrics and speaker names.
- Search Online looks on OpenSubtitles, Subdl, Podnapisi and your Jellyfin server at once, best match first. Subdl needs a free key in Settings › Subtitles, where each site can also be turned off.
- Fixed a dark copy of the first letters showing behind VLC's subtitles.

### Player
- Skip Intro and Skip Recap show on their own for 8 seconds, then only while the controls are up. Return still skips. Next Episode at the credits is unchanged.
- On the Mac, a click on the video plays or pauses. Double-click is still full screen.
- New keys: ⇧← and ⇧→ go to the previous or next subtitle line, C turns subtitles off and back on (crop moves to ⇧C), 0–9 jump to 0–90%, and < > step a frame.
- Fixed playback sticking on "Buffering" after stepping frame by frame, even with the whole file downloaded.

### Updates (Mac)
- Checking for, downloading and installing an update happens in place in Settings › About, with no popups. You see the size, What's New, progress with time left, and a clear reason if something fails. A background check shows a blue dot on the profile button instead of interrupting.
- The updater installs only a Bluebox signed with Bluebox's release certificate, checks the download against GitHub's checksum, and puts the old version back if the new one fails its check.

### Security
- Sign-in links no longer sign you in. Bluebox doesn't send sign-in emails, so a link like that can only come from someone else.
- Signing in to a different account asks what happens to this device's library before anything moves.
- API keys sync between devices only with Sync API Keys on (Settings › Account), which is off by default. Remove Keys from Server deletes them from your Supabase project.
- Links of add-ons you added by link, which often hold a debrid key, are kept in the Keychain, not in preferences or settings sync.
- Remote control uses private channels only your account can join. If you run your own Supabase project, run Set Up again (or Check Setup) to add the new policies, then turn off Realtime › Settings › Allow public access.
- A connect link opened from outside asks before pointing Bluebox at its server.
- Very large numbers from an add-on can no longer crash the app.
- SECURITY.md says how to report a vulnerability privately.

## 1.0.14 (30 October 2026)

### Mac
- Installing from the DMG: a new window, Bluebox's black and blue with an arrow from the app to Applications, the version, and what to do if macOS won't open Bluebox the first time. The disk shows Bluebox's icon.

### Episodes
- A watched episode shows a full blue bar under its picture, the same as one you've started, instead of a WATCHED tag.

### Fixed
- Updating from inside the Mac app failed with "hdiutil: attach failed - Operation not permitted". macOS keeps other programs out of Bluebox's own folder, so the updater now gets the download as an open file. Bluebox 1.0.13 and earlier still have the old updater: download 1.0.14 from the Releases page once, and updates work from then on.
- After an update installed, the old Bluebox never quit, so the new one never opened. Erase All Data had the same hang.

## 1.0.13 (30 October 2026)

### Player
- Picking a subtitle track or changing how subtitles look never reopens the video, so there's no rebuffer. Colour, background, outline, size and position show from the next line; font, weight and shadow take effect from the next video.
- Picking a subtitle no longer switches the audio to the file's default track.
- Opening the subtitle card no longer stutters: the track list isn't rebuilt several times a second, and ASS subtitles no longer load about 140 MB of Chinese, Japanese and Korean fonts each time they start.
- A simpler Subtitle Style card: presets, then Size, Colour, Background, Outline and Position.
- Every subtitle language is in one list that scrolls inside the card.
- Tools is three columns on a phone.
- Subtitles from files and subtitles in the video use the same font.
- Infuse as an external player: Open in Infuse on each release and finished download, and Settings › Player › Always Open in Infuse (this device only).

### Downloads
- A show's page has one ↓ button beside the season, with a menu: Download Season, Unwatched Only, As One Season Pack, and Download Rules…. While the season downloads, one thin progress line sits under the picker, with Pause and Cancel.
- Download Rules has no Use Defaults / Custom switch. Changing anything gives the show its own rules, and Reset to Settings goes back. Keep Next, Quality and Audio open a list instead of a pop-up.

### Fixed
- Torrents that reached "ready" but wouldn't play after the app had been in the background. iOS could close the stream server's socket while the app was suspended; the server now checks itself before each stream and listens again on the same port.
- Titles saved while using one catalog (TMDB or Cinemeta) still open, with their progress, downloads and My List, after switching to the other.
- A changelog, on the front page of the public repository.

## 1.0.11 (29 October 2026)

- Mac builds are signed with a stable certificate, so Always Allow lasts across updates.
- Mac updates: the app downloads the update itself, with progress over the window and in Settings › About, and gives up after 30 seconds without data. The helper only mounts, checks and swaps, with a time limit on each step, and says when macOS App Management blocks it.
- A subtitle change that reopens the stream keeps the audio track that was playing.

## 1.0.10 (29 October 2026)

- Mac player: changing subtitle size no longer reopens the stream, and never crashes. Font, colour and outline changes apply once the subtitle panel closes (one reopen, not one per tweak).

## 1.0.9 (29 October 2026)

The first release from the open-source repository, under the MIT licence. Builds 1.0.1 to 1.0.8 came out before the public history started over.

### Highlights
- **Home:** Top Picks, Because You Watched, Continue Watching that goes on to the next episode, and Not Interested.
- **Profiles:** Kids profiles and maturity limits, PIN lock, per-profile settings, and a Profiles menu on the Mac.
- **Lists:** a Library grid, list pages, Add to List, smart lists and Play All, synced between devices.
- **Search:** stable sections, filter chips, recent searches with pictures, and instant results from the cache and your library.
- **Release picker:** quality groups, chips and tags, with duplicates folded together.
- **Downloads:** whole seasons and season packs, a queue screen, show pages, and Download Rules.
- **Smart Downloads:** per-show rules, caps, a download window, upgrades, subtitles, and deleting watched episodes while keeping the next ones ready on Wi-Fi.
- **Player:** Audio & Subtitles picker with clean track names, an episodes panel, styled subtitles, torrent availability on the timeline, and anchored cards for tracks, speed and tools.
- **Sync:** API keys, add-ons, settings and lists sync through your own Supabase project, live. Sign in with a username and password, set up from one access token, and add another device.
- **Live Activity** for downloads on iPhone, a downloads item in the Mac menu bar, and iPhone as a remote for the Mac player.
- **My Bluebox:** watch statistics, a spoiler guard, always-skip per show, and release alerts.
- **Updates:** checks GitHub's latest release once a day and from Settings › About. The Mac installs it and opens again; iPhone and iPad point to AltStore.
- **Settings › Storage:** Clear Search History and Erase All Data.
- A custom About window on the Mac.

### Fixed
- Torrent downloads keep running in the background on iPhone and iPad.
- Home retries dropped requests and keeps the lists that did load.
- Searches no longer get stuck.
- The Mac app always opens with its main window, and Window › Bluebox (⌘0) brings it back.
- The Mac app no longer hangs at launch (the menu-bar downloads item rebuilt the window in a loop), and a Keychain prompt can no longer freeze it before the window appears.
- The player no longer hangs when stretching video.
- The Dynamic Island stays hidden while the player is up.

Installing#

My Mac won't open Bluebox
Click Done on the warning, then System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. Once per version. Step by step →
It says Bluebox “is damaged and can't be opened”
The download didn't finish properly. Delete it and download it again from the Releases page. Check the download →
My Mac keeps asking for my password
Type your Mac password and click Always Allow, not Allow. With the latest Bluebox it then asks only once. Full steps →
The update won't install on my Mac
Turn on Bluebox in System Settings › Privacy & Security › App Management and try again. Or quit, download the new version and drag it into Applications; your library stays. Full steps →
Bluebox stopped opening on my iPhone
With a free Apple ID, apps expire after 7 days. Open AltStore or SideStore, go to My Apps and tap Refresh All. Nothing is lost. Full steps →
Nothing happens when I tap Open in AltStore
Open the add page on the iPhone itself, with AltStore installed and opened once. Or tap Copy source link and add it under Sources in your app. Full steps →
Can I use Bluebox on an Intel Mac, Apple TV, Android or Windows?
Not yet: iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later, and Macs with Apple chips on macOS 15 or later. What you need →

Playing#

Nothing plays, or it keeps loading
Open Releases and pick another one, near the top or marked Cached. Still stuck? Try another network. Full steps →
It keeps stopping to buffer
Pick a smaller release (1080p or 720p). A debrid service almost never buffers. Full steps →
Will my file play?
Almost certainly: MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WebM and most other video files. Only AirPlay needs MP4 or MOV. Full steps →
I can't AirPlay to my TV
AirPlay needs an MP4 or MOV. Tap the greyed-out button: Bluebox offers a version that works. Full steps →
The picture freezes but the sound keeps going
Update Bluebox (Settings › About); newer versions fix this within a few seconds. Still happening with one film? Pick another release. Full steps →

Subtitles#

Subtitles don't match what people say
In the subtitle card, tap Timing, then Sync to the Audio. Way off? Use Search Online for ones made for your version. Full steps →
I can't find subtitles in my language
Tap Search Online in the subtitle card. A free OpenSubtitles key in Settings › Subtitles finds many more. Full steps →
Some subtitles won't change size or move
Fancy and Blu-ray subtitles keep their own look. Pick a plain track in the same language (often SRT), or one from Search Online. Full steps →

Debrid#

My debrid service shows an error
Bluebox names the problem. Usually: copy your key again, renew your plan, or pick a release marked Cached. Every message →
Nothing shows as Cached
Turn on Check What's Cached in Settings › Torrents & Debrid. Using StremThru or Comet? Also turn on Share Key with Community Add-ons. Full steps →

iPhone remote#

My iPhone can't find my Mac
Sign in to the same account on both, turn on Allow Remote Control of This Device on the Mac, keep Bluebox open there, then pick it in Play On. Same Wi-Fi isn't needed. Full steps →

Watch Party#

My friends can't join my party
Send the link from Share Invite…, not the code, and turn on Allow anonymous sign-ins in your Supabase dashboard. Each friend needs the same film in their Bluebox. Full steps →
The invite link says it's incomplete
Part of the link was cut off. Ask for it again and open the whole link. Full steps →

Downloads#

Downloads stop when I leave Bluebox on my iPhone
Debrid downloads carry on. Torrents need Bluebox running: keep Keep Downloading in the Background on and don't swipe Bluebox away. Nothing is lost if iOS closes it. Full steps →
My downloads say Waiting
It says why: waiting for Wi-Fi (Settings › Mobile Data), for your Download Window, or for one of the two at a time to finish. Full steps →
There's no way to download this one
Some streams can't be saved. In Releases, touch and hold (or right-click) another one and choose Download. Full steps →

Account and everything else#

Do I need an account?
No. Only to sync devices, use the remote or host a Watch Party. Bluebox sets one up for free. Set one up →
I forgot my password
Sign in to supabase.com, open your Bluebox project, and set a new one under Authentication › Users. Full steps →
Sync stopped working
Free Supabase projects sleep after a week unused. Tap Restore on supabase.com, then Sync Now in Bluebox. Full steps →
I forgot a profile's PIN
Tap Forgot PIN?, confirm with Touch ID, Face ID or the device password, then change the lock in Settings › Profiles. Full steps →
How do I report a bug?
Update first. Then Export Logs in Settings › Advanced (no passwords or keys are included) and open a GitHub issue with what happened. Security problems: report privately. Open an issue →
How do I start fresh?
In Settings › Storage: Reset All Settings keeps your lists and downloads; Erase All Data removes everything from this device. Full steps →
Does Bluebox come with films and shows?
No. It's a player, like a web browser for video. What you watch comes from sources you choose, and it's up to you to have the right to watch it. Full steps →
Can I use Bluebox in my language?
Yes: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese (Settings › Language). Full steps →

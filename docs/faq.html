Help and FAQ

Pick what's going wrong, then follow the steps. Most problems are fixed in a minute or two.

Installing#

My Mac won't open Bluebox

The first time you open a new version, macOS shows a warning because Bluebox isn't from the App Store. You only have to get past it once.

  1. Click Done on the warning.
  2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and scroll down.
  3. Click Open Anyway next to Bluebox, enter your Mac password, then click Open Anyway once more.

No Open Anyway button? It only shows for about an hour. Open Bluebox again, click Done, and go straight back to Privacy & Security.

It says Bluebox “is damaged and can't be opened”

The download didn't finish properly.

  1. Drag Bluebox from Applications to the Bin.
  2. Download it again from the official Releases page, and drag it into Applications.
  3. Open it and follow the steps in My Mac won't open Bluebox.

Still says damaged? Check the download is genuine, then see the Install page for the last-resort fix.

My Mac keeps asking for my password

That's macOS asking whether Bluebox may read its saved passwords.

  1. Type your Mac password and click Always Allow (not Allow).
  2. Make sure you have the latest Bluebox. It then asks only once, even after updates.
The update won't install on my Mac
  1. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › App Management and turn on Bluebox. Then try again in Settings › About.
  2. Still failing? Install it by hand: quit Bluebox, download the newest version from the Releases page and drag it into Applications, replacing the old one. Your library and settings stay.
Bluebox stopped opening on my iPhone

If you installed it with a free Apple ID, Apple makes it expire after 7 days.

  1. Open AltStore or SideStore.
  2. Go to My Apps and tap Refresh All.

Your library and downloads are kept. Leave your sideloading app's background refresh on so this happens by itself.

Nothing happens when I tap Open in AltStore
  1. Open the add page on your iPhone or iPad, not on a computer.
  2. Make sure AltStore (or SideStore, or Feather) is installed and you've opened it once.
  3. Still nothing? Tap Copy source link, then in your sideloading app go to Sources, tap + and paste.
Can I use Bluebox on an Intel Mac, Apple TV, Android or Windows?

Not yet. Bluebox runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS 18 or later) and on Macs with Apple chips (M1 or newer, macOS 15 or later).

Playing#

Nothing plays, or it keeps loading

Go through these one at a time. Most people are watching by step 2.

Step 1 of 5

Pick a different version

On the title's page, open Releases and choose another one. Pick one near the top with lots of people sharing it, or one marked Cached if you use a debrid service.

Step 2 of 5

Try another network

Some Wi-Fi networks (work, school, hotels) and some phone carriers block the kind of sharing Bluebox uses. Try your home Wi-Fi, a different network, or a VPN.

Step 3 of 5

Check your add-ons are answering

Go to Settings › Torrents & Debrid and tap Test Add-ons. Turn off any that fail.

Step 4 of 5

On mobile data?

Open Settings › Mobile Data and make sure streaming is allowed.

Step 5 of 5

Using your own server or files?

Go to Settings › Sources and tap Test All Sources. It tells you which one Bluebox can't reach, so you can check that computer or NAS is on.

Enjoy the film.

Still not playing? Some titles only have versions nobody is sharing right now. A debrid service fixes most of these. Otherwise tell us about it.

It keeps stopping to buffer
  1. Open Releases and pick a smaller version: 1080p or 720p instead of 4K, with lots of people sharing it.
  2. Let it buffer longer before starting: in Settings › Torrents & Debrid, raise Start Playing After.

A debrid service streams from fast servers and almost never buffers.

Will my file play?

Almost certainly. Bluebox plays MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WebM, TS and most other video files.

The one catch: AirPlay and picture in picture only work with MP4 and MOV. See I can't AirPlay to my TV.

I can't AirPlay to my TV

AirPlay works with MP4 and MOV videos. The button is greyed out for other kinds, like MKV.

  1. Tap the greyed-out AirPlay button. Bluebox offers a version of the same film that does work with AirPlay.
  2. Or open Releases and pick one marked MP4.
The picture freezes but the sound keeps going
  1. Update Bluebox (Settings › About). Newer versions fix this by themselves within a few seconds.
  2. Still happens with one film? Pick a different version in Releases.

Subtitles#

Subtitles don't match what people say
  1. While watching, open the subtitles button, then Timing.
  2. Tap Sync to the Audio at the top. Bluebox listens for a moment and fixes the timing for you. (The first time, it asks to use speech recognition.)
  3. Not quite right? Tap the line you just heard in the list, and Bluebox lines it up.

Way off and nothing helps? The subtitles were made for a different version. Use Search Online to get ones that match.

I can't find subtitles in my language
  1. While watching, open the subtitles button and tap Search Online.
  2. For many more results, add a free OpenSubtitles key in Settings › Subtitles. (Make one at opensubtitles.com, under Profile › API consumers.)
  3. To have your language turned on by itself, set it in Settings › Language.
Some subtitles won't change size or move

Fancy subtitles (coloured signs, karaoke) and the picture-style ones from Blu-rays keep their own look.

  1. Open the subtitles button and pick a plain track in the same language (often labelled SRT), or one from Search Online.

Those follow your size, colour and position.

Debrid#

My debrid service shows an error

Bluebox tells you what's wrong in a message like this, with a button to fix it:

The message saysDo this
Key not acceptedCopy your key again from your service's website and paste it in Settings › Torrents & Debrid. Check the right service is picked.
Plan expiredRenew on the service's website. Turn on Remind Before the Plan Ends so it doesn't surprise you again.
Not ready yetYour service is still fetching it. Tap Releases… and pick one marked Cached, or try again later.
Limit reachedYou've used today's allowance. Wait a while, or clear old items on the service's website.
Nothing shows as Cached
  1. In Settings › Torrents & Debrid, turn on Check What's Cached.
  2. If you use StremThru or Comet, also turn on Share Key with Community Add-ons.

Less popular films may just not be cached yet. Playing one asks your service to fetch it.

iPhone remote#

My iPhone can't find my Mac
Step 1 of 4

Sign in on both

The remote needs both devices signed in to the same Bluebox account. Open Settings › Account & Sync on each and check the username matches. No account yet? Set one up first.

Step 2 of 4

Let the Mac be controlled

On the Mac, turn this on. It's off until you do.

Step 3 of 4

Pick the Mac on your iPhone

Keep Bluebox open on the Mac. On the iPhone, tap Play On at the top and choose your Mac.

Step 4 of 4

Update both

Install the latest Bluebox on the Mac and the iPhone (Settings › About).

You're in control. Swipe to move, tap to choose.

Still can't connect? If Settings › Account & Sync says “Remote control and instant sync are off”, tap Check Setup there. Otherwise see more fixes or tell us.

The devices don't need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

Watch Party#

My friends can't join my party
  1. Send them the link from Share Invite…, not just the code. The code only works for people on your own account.
  2. Let guests in: in your Supabase dashboard, go to Authentication › Sign In / Providers, turn on Allow anonymous sign-ins, and save. You only do this once.
  3. Keep Bluebox open while the party's on.

Friend joined but can't watch? Everyone plays their own copy, so they need the same film in their Bluebox: the same add-ons or debrid service as you. The invitation tells them if they're ready.

The invite link says it's incomplete

Part of the link got cut off when it was copied. Ask your friend to send it again with Share Invite…, and open the whole link.

Downloads#

Downloads stop when I leave Bluebox on my iPhone

Downloads from a debrid service carry on by themselves. Torrent downloads need Bluebox to stay running.

  1. Check this is on:
  2. Don't swipe Bluebox away in the app switcher. That stops it.
  3. For a big download, plug the phone in and leave Bluebox open on the Downloads page.

If iOS closes Bluebox anyway, nothing is lost: downloads carry on from where they stopped next time you open it.

My downloads say Waiting

The Downloads page says what each one is waiting for. Usually it's one of these:

  1. Waiting for Wi-Fi: downloads don't use mobile data unless you allow it in Settings › Mobile Data.
  2. Waiting for overnight: you've set a Download Window in Settings › Downloads.
  3. Queued: two download at a time. The rest start when one finishes.
There's no way to download this one

Some live-style streams can't be saved. Open Releases, touch and hold (or right-click) a different version, and choose Download.

Account and everything else#

Do I need an account?

No. You only need one to sync between devices, use the iPhone remote, or host a Watch Party. Bluebox can set one up for you in about two minutes, for free.

I forgot my password
  1. Sign in to supabase.com and open your Bluebox project.
  2. Go to Authentication › Users and find your username.
  3. Set a new password there, then sign in to Bluebox with it.
Sync stopped working

Free Supabase projects go to sleep after a week of not being used.

  1. Sign in to supabase.com and tap Restore on your Bluebox project.
  2. In Bluebox, tap Sync Now in Settings › Account & Sync.
I forgot a profile's PIN
  1. Tap Forgot PIN? on the PIN screen.
  2. Confirm with Touch ID, Face ID or the device's password.
  3. In Settings › Profiles, change or remove the lock.
How do I report a bug?
  1. Update to the latest version and check it still happens.
  2. Make it happen once more, then go to Settings › Advanced and tap Export Logs. (Passwords and keys are never included.)
  3. Open an issue on GitHub: say what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Attach the log and a screenshot if you can.

Found a security problem? Please report it privately instead.

How do I start fresh?

Go to Settings › Storage:

  1. Reset All Settings puts settings back but keeps your lists and downloads.
  2. Erase All Data removes everything from this device. Bluebox closes; open it again to finish.
Does Bluebox come with films and shows?

No. Bluebox is a player, like a web browser for video. What you watch comes from the add-ons, servers and files you choose, and it's up to you to have the right to watch it.

Can I use Bluebox in my language?

Yes: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. Choose in Settings › Language.

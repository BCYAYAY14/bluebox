Install

Free, from GitHub. Not on the App Store.

Mac

  1. Download, then drag Bluebox to Applications.
  2. Open it and click Done on the warning.
  3. System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.
Download for MacStep by step

iPhone and iPad

  1. On your iPhone, open the add page.
  2. Pick AltStore, SideStore or Feather (or copy the link).
  3. Tap Get on Bluebox. Updates come the same way.

Bluebox is a player. What you watch comes from the add-ons, servers and peers you choose, and you're responsible for having the right to watch it. Torrents share your IP address with other people; a debrid service or a VPN avoids that. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

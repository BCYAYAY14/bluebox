Install

Bluebox isn't on the App Store. It's one free build for every device, from GitHub.

Mac

  1. Download the DMG and drag Bluebox to Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS can't check it, so click Done.
  3. In System Settings › Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway. Once per version.
Download for MacFull steps

iPhone and iPad

  1. On your iPhone, open the add page and pick AltStore, SideStore or Feather.
  2. Using FlareStore or ESign? Copy the source link there and add it under Sources.
  3. Open Bluebox in the source and tap Get. Updates arrive the same way.

Good to know

Bluebox is a player. What you watch comes from the add-ons, servers and peers you choose, and you're responsible for having the right to watch it. Torrents share your IP address with everyone in the swarm; a debrid service or a VPN avoids that. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
